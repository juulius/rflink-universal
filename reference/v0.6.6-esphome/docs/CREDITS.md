# How RFLink Universal was built

## Human-driven. AI-assisted.

RFLink Universal started from a practical question: could several RF bands and protocols be combined into one extensible, approachable device?

**Juulius — creator and hardware designer:** project concept, functional direction, requirements, hardware design decisions, PCB design and board layout, iterative feedback, and independent enclosure design (still in progress).

**ChatGPT by OpenAI — AI development assistance:** a large part of the firmware, architectural options, debugging assistance, tests, documentation and repeated reviews of hardware/PCB decisions. Juulius directed the work and evaluated the proposed implementations. The tools and source material used by independent projects retain their own licenses and credits.
