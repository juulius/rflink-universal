# RFLink Universal UX principles

1. Simple by default, powerful under Advanced.
2. Ask only for information RFLink cannot safely infer.
3. Never silently overwrite learned/imported user work.
4. Never require protocol knowledge for normal device learning.
5. Explain what happened and what to do next; hide implementation details by default.
6. Required is amber + text/icon; errors are red + a concrete recovery action.
7. `?` help is contextual, short and available where jargon appears.
8. One primary task per screen; one selected decoder/profile detail at a time.
9. Safe defaults are preselected, but behavior-changing migrations still require confirmation.
10. Advanced can expose raw RF data, IDs, versions, fingerprints and diagnostics without changing normal behavior.
11. Import/export always previews before applying and must be recoverable on failure.
12. Mobile gets the same capability, not a reduced technical subset.
13. Normal screens use short labels and at most one short explanation per decision; longer reasoning belongs under Advanced or contextual help.
