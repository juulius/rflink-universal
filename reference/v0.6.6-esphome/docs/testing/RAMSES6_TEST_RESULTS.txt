Safety behavior:
- uart_stream and SPI FIFO are receive-only experimental modes.
- Legacy Itho retains control at boot.
- Optional automatic time-sharing and all diagnostic radio leases default OFF.
- UART startup failure permits fallback only after verified Itho restoration;
  failed restore is sticky FAULT and denies TX/RX use of the affected radio.
